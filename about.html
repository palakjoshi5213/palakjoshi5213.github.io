I am an architect with a keen eye for detail and a commitment to doing work well. I am drawn to how architectural space is shaped and how it is built, and I often work out ideas in sketches and illustrations long before they become drawings.

I graduated from Aditya College of Architecture in 2024, first in my class with a CGPA of 8.99, after a thesis on regenerating the inner-city core of Bhuleshwar in Mumbai. Internships with Amar Tendulkar Architects, Sandeep Shikre Architects and Ar. Shruti Pandit took me from concept presentations to construction documentation, site visits and green building certification. Since October 2024 I have worked as a Junior Architect and Medical Planner at Language.Architecture.Body in Mumbai.

I believe architecture should do more than function. Spaces can be open, legible and good to be in; they can encourage interaction, support well-being and sit comfortably within their surroundings. That is the architecture I want to build.
